classic-man.glb and classic-woman.glb are built by tools/make_people.py from MakeHuman's
assets (the base mesh, its shape targets, the Mixamo-named skeleton and its skin weights,
and the masks used to paint the skin), as carried by MPFB2:
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2
MakeHuman's assets are released under CC0 1.0 Universal: anyone may use them for anything.
So are these two models.

man.glb and woman.glb are two avatars from the Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library
(Male_Adult_02 and Female_Adult_02), https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox,
rebuilt for Riptide by tools/make_people_rocketbox.js: turned to face +Z in metres, the
arms let down to the sides, the skeleton renamed the way Mixamo names it, and the
textures resized.

rocketbox/*.glb are twelve more avatars from the same library (Female_Adult_01, 04, 05,
08 and 10, and Male_Adult_01, 03, 05, 06, 08, 10 and 12), rebuilt the same way by
tools/make_people_more.py. They are covered by the same MIT License, below.
